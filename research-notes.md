# Public source notes — Museum Sejarah Jakarta

Checked 19 October 2026. Independent guide; museum and Taman Fatahillah square are separate entities.

- National registry: https://museum.kemenbud.go.id/museum/profile/museum%2Bsejarah%2Bjakarta — Tuesday–Friday 09:00–15:00; Saturday–Sunday 09:00–20:00; Monday closed. Weekday Rp10,000 and weekend Rp15,000 shown without visitor-category detail beside those amounts. Attribute as reference figures; confirm categories and current fees.
- Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/XaSitKkU9B9Wd5Lf6 — snapshot 19 October 2026, 4.6/5 from 22,916 reviews; Tuesday–Sunday 09:00–15:00, Monday closed; +62216929101; pin -6.1351828,106.8133498; Taman Fatahillah No.1, Pinangsia, Taman Sari, Jakarta Barat 11110. Third-party dated listing, not the guide’s own rating.
- Older cultural database: https://budaya.data.kemdikbud.go.id/lembaga/objek/MS000056 — Tuesday–Sunday 09:00–15:00 and same phone. Weekend times conflict with national registry; visitors should confirm before arriving after 15:00 and on holidays. No unconditional opening-hours schema is emitted.
- Jakarta Culture Office: https://dinaskebudayaan.jakarta.go.id/museum/museum-sejarah-jakarta/548/4 — city development, archaeology, antique furniture, ceramics, inscriptions. No invented gallery inventory, interviews or archival identifiers.
- Jakarta provincial government: https://retribusi.jakarta.go.id/informasi-terkini/museum-sejarah-jakarta-objek-retribusi — 30 March 1974 museum opening. Its 2023 Rp5,000 figure is historical, not current pricing evidence.
